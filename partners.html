Who we partner with

Technology and integrations

ELDs and telematics, load tracking, load boards, accounting and other software. Your data shows up where dispatchers already work, through the Harlo API or a built-in connection in Settings, Integrations.

Factoring and financial services

Harlo already knows when a load is delivered, invoiced and paid. Offer factoring, fuel cards or payments to carriers at the moment they need them, with paperwork that's already complete.

Insurance

Carriers keep their equipment, drivers and authority in Harlo. Help them get covered, and stay covered, without retyping the fleet.

Resellers, consultants and referrals

Set carriers up on Harlo, train their teams, or simply point the people you advise our way. Associations, schools and communities welcome.

Building an integration? The Harlo API is documented at developer.goharlo.com, and any Harlo customer can make a key today. Partners get a test workspace to build against.
