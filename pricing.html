Billing questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. You only add a card when you choose a paid plan.
What happens when the 14 days are up?
Your workspace moves to Free and nothing is deleted. Over Free's limits, everything stays readable and exportable, but adding loads, trucks or users needs a paid plan.
What counts as a truck?
An active power unit. Trailers, drivers and users are never billed. Trucks added mid-month are prorated.
Do the apps cost extra?
No. Every plan includes desktop and Harlo Go. Pro and up add Harlo Driver.
What's launch pricing?
Start Pro, Fleet, Broker or Broker Scale before March 1, 2027 and pay the launch price for your first 12 months: $39 a truck instead of $49 on Pro, $32 instead of $40 from truck 26, $399 instead of $499 on Broker, $999 instead of $1,249 on Broker Scale. Yearly plans too. Moving up a plan keeps it.
What counts as a load for a brokerage?
A brokered load covered by a carrier during the month. Quotes, cancelled loads, and loads covered by your own fleet on Carrier + Broker don't count.
What if my brokerage goes over its included loads?
Nothing stops. Extra loads bill at $2.00 on Broker or $1.50 on Broker Scale. Above about 500 loads a month, Scale is cheaper.
How is the Carrier + Broker plan priced?
Carrier side: $49 a truck ($40 from truck 26), or $39 ($32) at launch for your first year, no minimum. Brokerage side: the broker plan that fits your volume, 25% off. One login, one bill.
What's an AI credit?
One task for Ike, like reading a document or answering a question. Every plan includes a monthly allowance. Free stops at its allowance; Pro and up can go over at 5¢ a credit (4¢ on Fleet), up to a hard ceiling. Now and then we raise every allowance for a while, at no charge.
Can I pay yearly?
Yes. Twelve months for the price of ten. Switch any time in billing settings.
Is there a contract?
No. Cancel any time in billing settings. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
Do my drivers need to install an app?
No. Drivers can get load details by text. On Pro and up they can also use Harlo Driver on a phone or the truck's tablet.
Who can see my data?
Only people in your company. Every account is isolated in the database and every change is logged. Harlo trains its own models on activity across the platform, with personal details removed, so it gets better for everyone. We never sell your data, and no other customer sees your rates, margins, customers, drivers or loads. Read the privacy policy for the details.
