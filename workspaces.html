Workspace questions

Can people in one workspace see another?
Only if they're invited. People see only their workspaces, with the role you give them in each.
Can one person have different roles in different workspaces?
Yes. An admin in one, a viewer in another.
How does a load move from my brokerage to my fleet?
Tender it to your own carrier workspace. It arrives booked, with the rate con attached, and each side keeps its own money.
Can I start with one workspace and add more later?
Yes. Add one whenever you start a new authority, terminal or brokerage.
