Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

Harlo Solutions LLC (“ Harlo Solutions ”, “we”) provides operational software to trucking companies. This policy explains what information the Harlo platform handles, why, and who it is shared with. It covers our website and the Harlo application.

1. Our role: carriers control their own data

Almost everything in Harlo is entered by a motor carrier about its own business — its drivers, its equipment, its customers, its loads. For that information the carrier is the controller and Harlo Solutions is a processor: we hold and process it on the carrier's instructions, under our agreement with them, and we do not use it for our own purposes.

If you are a driver, employee, or contact of a carrier that uses Harlo and you want to see, correct, or delete information about you, contact that carrier first — they decide what their records say. We will help them respond, and we will pass requests we receive directly along to them.

We are the controller for a smaller set of data: the accounts of the people who sign in, billing records, support correspondence, and the operational logs we keep to run the service.

2. Information in the platform

  • Account and sign-in data — name, work email, role and permissions, authentication records, and sign-in activity.
  • Carrier operational data — customers, facilities, loads, routes, dispatch, equipment, financial records, and the event history of changes to them.
  • Driver information— name, date of birth, contact details, address, employment records, pay terms, commercial driver's licence and endorsements, medical examiner's certificates, incidents, and duty and schedule records. Some of this is personal and sensitive; it is in Harlo because DOT and FMCSA rules require a carrier to keep it.
  • Documents — files uploaded by the carrier, such as rate confirmations, bills of lading, licences, and certificates, together with the data extracted from them.
  • Mobile numbers and messaging consent— where a carrier enables text notifications, the driver's mobile number, whether they consented, the exact wording they agreed to, and when. See our SMS consent disclosure.
  • Technical and usage data — IP address, browser and device information, pages and features used, and error and performance logs.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. Harlo is business software and is not directed at children.

3. How the information is used

  • Running the platform: showing a carrier its own operation, and recording the facts it enters.
  • Producing the analysis Harlo exists for — compliance and fitness conclusions, risk and exception signals, document understanding, and the assistant's answers.
  • Sending operational notifications, including text messages to drivers who have consented.
  • Keeping an audit trail of changes, which we retain because carriers are subject to audit.
  • Security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and support.
  • Billing, and measuring usage against a subscription.
  • Improving Harlo — using aggregated and de-identified statistics that do not identify a carrier, a driver, or a load.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use carrier data to advertise to anyone.

4. Automated analysis and AI

Harlo interprets operational data — it reads uploaded documents, derives compliance conclusions, and answers questions about a carrier's operation. Some of that runs on models we host ourselves, and some, where a carrier has it enabled, runs on third-party model providers we contract with.

Where a third-party provider is used, it processes the content only to return a result to us. Carrier data is not used to train third-party models.

Harlo's conclusions are decision support, not decisions. Anything that changes a carrier's records is proposed to a person and applied only once they approve it, and the approval is logged.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers who help us run Harlo, under contracts limiting them to that purpose — hosting and infrastructure, network and security providers, text-message delivery (Twilio), AI model providers where enabled, mapping and geocoding, and payment processing.
  • The carrier whose account the data belongs to, and the users it has authorised.
  • Legal and safety — where we are required by law, or where it is necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service. Where we may lawfully do so, we will tell the affected carrier first.
  • A successor, if the business is acquired or merged. This policy continues to apply to information transferred that way.

6. Retention

Operational and audit records are kept for as long as the carrier's account is active, because the carrier needs them and is often required to keep them. When an account closes, the carrier may export its data; we then delete or de-identify it within 90 days, except where we must keep something longer to meet a legal, tax, or regulatory obligation, or to resolve a dispute. Backups age out on their own schedule.

7. Security

Access to a carrier's data is restricted at the database level, so one carrier cannot read another's. Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role, changes are recorded in an audit trail, and administrative access is limited to staff who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information we will notify you and the relevant regulators as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing (we do neither), and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other states with comparable laws have these rights; so do people covered by the UK and EU GDPR.

Write to support@goharlo.com and we will respond within the time the applicable law allows. Where the request concerns data a carrier controls, we will forward it to that carrier and support their response rather than acting on their records ourselves.

9. Text messages

Mobile numbers collected for driver notifications are used only to send that driver operational messages from their own carrier. They are never sold, rented, or shared with anyone for marketing. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by asking the carrier to switch notifications off. Our full disclosure is at /sms-consent.

10. Where data is held

Harlo is operated from the United States and information is processed there. If you use it from elsewhere, you are sending information to the United States, where privacy law differs from your own.

11. Changes

We will update this policy as Harlo changes, and revise the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we handle personal information we will tell account administrators before it takes effect.
