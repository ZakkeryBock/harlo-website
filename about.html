We ran a trucking company first.

Harlo started as the system that ran a small carrier, built by the people doing its dispatch and paperwork. Now it gives every carrier that edge.

  1. Before Harlo

    Fat Cat Freight, a small carrier, runs on Loadbar: a system pieced together in Bubble and Airtable because nothing on the market fit.

  2. 2023

    Fat Cat Freight closes. The lessons from running it, and Loadbar itself, don't.

  3. Rebuild

    Loadbar is rebuilt from scratch for other carriers and becomes Harlo, with an assistant that reads the paperwork.

  4. Today

    Harlo is open to every carrier, free for up to two trucks, with Harlo Go for owner-operators running from their phone.
