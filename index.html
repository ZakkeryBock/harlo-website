Is Harlo for carriers or brokers?
Both. Run either one, or both side by side as separate workspaces under one login.
How long does setup take?
Most teams run their first load the same day. There's no implementation project.
What's a workspace?
One operation — a carrier, a brokerage, an authority or a terminal — with its own loads, people and settings. Switch between them in a click.
Do I need a credit card to start?
No. You only add a card when you choose a paid plan.
What happens when the 14 days are up?
Your workspace moves to Free and nothing is deleted. Over Free's limits, everything stays readable and exportable, but adding loads, trucks or users needs a paid plan.
