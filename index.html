Who We Are

Built by people who know the work

Harlo is a small team rebuilding how a trucking company runs its day. The name stands for harmony — technology and operations working as one thing instead of two, so the software stops being another job on top of the job.

We are based in Mokena, Illinois, and we build for carriers: the dispatchers, the safety managers, and the people who answer the phone when a load is late.

What We Do

An operating system that pays attention

Harlo runs the whole operation — loads, dispatch, drivers, equipment, compliance, money — and then does the part other systems leave to you: it reads what is actually happening and surfaces what matters.

Dispatch & loads

Build a load, plan the route, assign the crew and the equipment, and track every stop as it happens.

Compliance that watches itself

Licences, medical cards, registrations and inspections are checked continuously. An expired CDL stops a dispatch before it leaves the yard.

Documents that read themselves

Upload a rate confirmation and the load is already built. No retyping what the paperwork already says.

Driver messaging

Drivers get their assignment as a text the moment it is dispatched, with consent recorded and every send logged.

Ike, in the corner

Ask about your operation in plain language. Ike answers from your live data, and never acts without your say-so.

An audit trail by default

Every change records who, what and when — because someone will eventually ask.
