Harlo gets better by learning how freight actually moves. Aggregated Data means information derived from Customer Data that has been combined with data from other customers and de-identified, so that it does not identify, and cannot reasonably be used to identify, a customer, a driver, an employee, a broker, a shipper, or an individual load, rate, or transaction.

The customer grants us a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free right to use Customer Data to create Aggregated Data, and to use, retain, publish, and commercialise that Aggregated Data — in benchmarks, industry statistics, product features, and models — during the term and after it ends. Aggregated Data is not Customer Data; as between the parties it is ours.

These limits bind us, and they are the point of the section:

  • We do not publish, sell, or disclose a customer's rates, margins, financial records, customer or broker relationships, driver records, or load-level detail in identifiable form to anyone.

  • Because Aggregated Data no longer identifies anyone, deleting Customer Data does not require us to unwind statistics or models already derived from it.
